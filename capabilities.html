Product Lanes

What we supply.

Every lane is sourced through authorized OEM or national-distributor channels and shipped directly to the receiving facility. Manufacturer authorization documentation is supplied for the solicitation record on request.

01

IT Hardware, Software & Cloud

Enterprise software licensing and renewals, SaaS and subscription services, cloud and hosting, servers and storage, workstations and laptops, networking and peripherals. Sourced through authorized distribution rather than the open market.

02

OEM Maintenance & Support Renewals

Manufacturer-certified hardware maintenance, warranty extensions, and support contract renewals for Dell EMC, HPE, Cisco, NetApp and comparable lines, so installed fleets stay covered without a lapse in entitlement.

03

Medical Equipment

Ultrasound and imaging, patient monitoring, EEG/ECG and electromedical apparatus, exam and treatment furniture, pharmacy refrigeration, and dental equipment for VA medical centers and federal health facilities.

04

Communications Systems

Land mobile radio (LMR/P25) hardware and accessories, satellite terminals, satellite airtime and service plans, and supporting infrastructure for federal facilities and field operations.

Also delivered today

Managed live-stream & broadcast. End-to-end streaming deployment and ongoing operation, covering encoding, distribution, and audience reporting. In continuous production since 2023 and still running.

This is where Lot 9’s technology work began, and it remains an active service line alongside the federal supply lanes above. We keep it affordable so that it stays within reach of the families and communities it serves. See the results below.

How We Work

From solicitation number to delivered goods.

A deliberately short path. Most requirements move from first email to submitted quote inside the response window without needing a follow-up call.

Step 01

Send the requirement

Solicitation number, line items, quantities, delivery location, and the response deadline. Brand-name-or-equal and salient characteristics noted if they apply.

Step 02

We source and confirm

We go to the authorized channel for that product line, confirm availability and lead time, and obtain manufacturer authorization documentation where the solicitation requires it.

Step 03

Quote and award

You receive a firm quote with sourcing confirmation attached. On award, goods ship direct from the authorized source to the receiving facility, FOB destination.
