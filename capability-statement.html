Who We Are

Lot 9 LLC is a Service-Disabled Veteran-Owned Small Business founded in October 2022. The company began serving the veteran community in e-commerce, expanded into local service operations, and moved into information technology through a managed IT and live-streaming engagement we still deliver today. Our federal focus is business-to-government technology resale. Lot 9 is primarily engaged in the retail and wholesale trade as an established dealer and reseller of IT hardware and software, communications and radio equipment, and medical equipment, supplied to federal agencies through authorized OEM and national distribution channels.

Our value is straightforward: we bring the catalogs of major OEMs and national distributors into set-aside channels those large firms cannot bid directly.

  • IT Hardware, Software & Cloud Resale: enterprise licensing, SaaS and subscription renewals, cloud services, and OEM hardware via authorized distribution

Differentiators

  • SBA VetCert-certified SDVOSB and VOSB: listed in the SBA certification database, not self-certified; eligible for VA Rule-of-Two set-asides under VAAR 852.219-73/74 and for SDVOSB, VOSB, and small-business set-asides
