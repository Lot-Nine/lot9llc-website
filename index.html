Our Foundation

Where the name comes from.

The LORD is the portion of mine inheritance and of my cup: thou maintainest my lot. The lines are fallen unto me in pleasant places; yea, I have a goodly heritage. Psalm 16:5–6 · KJV

Our name comes from scripture. A lot is a portion, a piece of ground measured out and given to someone to look after. We try to keep that in mind in how we run this company and how we treat the people we work with.

We are a veteran-owned business, and we believe in being there for people. That shows up in the ordinary things: doing what we said we would do, answering when you call, and treating a small order with the same care as a large one.

01

Integrity

We do what we say we will do. If we give you a price or a date, we stand behind it. If something cannot be done, we would rather tell you honestly than promise it anyway.

02

Stewardship

We try to take good care of what we are trusted with. Public money is being spent on every order, so we price it fairly, source it through authorized channels, and keep an honest record of the work.

03

Service

There is a real person and a real need at the end of every order, whether that is a VA medical center, a field unit, or a federal facility. We try to supply it the way we would want it supplied to us.
